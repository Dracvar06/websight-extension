WebSight Settings

Everything saves by itself as you change it.

1. The AI that reads pages for you

When a cloud AI is busy or at its limit, Ollama on this computer answers instead, if it is running.

Gemini

Key from Google AI Studio. A new key is on Google's free tier, which is often busy and may use what you send to improve Google's products. With a Google AI Pro plan, its monthly Cloud credit can pay for the faster paid tier (the setup guide shows how). Stored only on this computer.

Advanced: Gemini model

Default: gemini-3.8-flash, the current Flash in AI Studio. Press Test connection to see what your key can use.

Ollama (on this computer)

Needs the Ollama app running. Default: gemma4:e4b (install once in the Terminal with: ollama pull gemma4:e4b). Nothing leaves this computer.

Advanced: Ollama address

Default: http://localhost:11434

Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI or any other service

Pick a model that can see images, or WebSight answers from the page text alone. Press Test connection to list the models your key can use. "Any other AI service" works with almost any provider that offers an OpenAI-compatible API (Mistral, xAI, Together, LM Studio and many more): paste its address, ending in /v1. Free tiers have limits and may use what you send to improve their models. Keys: Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI. Stored only on this computer.

Claude

Key from platform.claude.com, paid per use. Default: claude-opus-5-5, the most capable ($4 per million tokens read). claude-haiku-5-5 costs far less ($0.10 per million read, $0.50 per million written) and handles most pages well. Stored only on this computer.

DeepSeek

Key from platform.deepseek.com. It cannot see screenshots, and what you send is processed in China. Stored only on this computer.

Advanced: DeepSeek model

Default: deepseek-v4-flash

2. About you

For example: "I am a blind student at UCSB. I use VoiceOver. I often use Técnico Lisboa's website. I speak English, Catalan and Spanish." WebSight gives this to the AI with every question, so its guesses fit you, and to the speech recognizer, so it spells your names right. You can also say "remember that..." while talking to it. It stays on this computer, except that a cloud AI receives it with each question.

3. Listening

Grant microphone access once here so the hotkey can listen right away.

If you always speak one language, choosing it helps short commands be heard right. Leave it on automatic if you mix languages.

Advanced: speech recognition

Whisper runs on the local voice server (free, private, no key) and records until you press Enter, however long you pause. It can also point at another Whisper server, such as Groq's cloud (address https://api.groq.com/openai/v1 with a free key). If that server does not answer, the local one is used.

Local server: deepdml/faster-whisper-large-v3-turbo-ct2. Groq: whisper-large-v3-turbo.

4. Voice

Many screen reader users prefer 2x or faster.

Neural voices sound natural in English, Spanish, Catalan, French, Italian, Portuguese, Hindi, Japanese and Chinese; other languages use system voices automatically.

Advanced: voice server and system voice

Automatic picks a system voice matching the answer's language, avoiding the low-quality novelty ones. Nicer system voices can be installed under macOS Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content; then reopen this page.

5. Finding pages on a site

"Take me to the admissions page", and questions whose answer is elsewhere on a site, use a search engine instead of clicking through menus. Only pages on the site you are on, or the official site Wikidata names, are ever opened, so a search can never take you to a lookalike. Searches carry only the site and your words. If the engine is not running or has no key, WebSight uses the site's own list of pages (its sitemap).

Advanced: search address and key

6. Behaviour

On every new page you hear a short description: what the page is and the two or three most useful things on it. The page is never scrolled.

7. Keyboard shortcuts

    If a shortcut says NOT ASSIGNED, Chrome dropped it (usually a conflict with another extension). Assign it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts: find WebSight, click the empty box and press the keys you want. On a Mac, Alt is the Option key.
